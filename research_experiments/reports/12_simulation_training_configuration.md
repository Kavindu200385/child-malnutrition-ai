# Simulation Training Configuration

Draft configuration: `configs/step2d_simulation_training_draft.json`. It defines Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost candidate settings without executing them. Both primary and no-`current_risk` ablation schemas are required. Preprocessing and balancing must be fitted only on the training partition.

Training may proceed only as a reproducible simulation-label experiment after approval. It cannot claim clinical prediction, observed two-month outcomes, temporal validation, external validation, or verified biological/WHO target correctness.
